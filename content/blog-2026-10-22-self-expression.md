On the occasion of starting this [[Blog]], with absolutely no time or ambition to do the advertising on social media so that anyone might actually find this stuff, the obvious question arises:

> What is the point?

_Why am I wasting my time doing such a thing?_

Similar to the first blog entry ([[2026-Oct-21: AI Software: Everyone gets their own toothbrush!]]), I guess I just have a particularly **solipsistic** bent!

I have always been very internally motivated -- extremely self-directed -- even back when I was a kid who just wanted to stay home and play with my Legos (which is now mirrored so clearly by my daughter).

And I have random thoughts.

And now I have a place to write down those thoughts.

_But why don't I just keep a private journal of all this garbage?_

Well, I do. But _these_ random thoughts benefit from the _notion_ that someone else might read them! Even if that is not the case! I write very different kinds of things to myself, only for myself.

_Am I satisfied with this justification?_

Yeah, because it is so _easy_ to do this, and, yes, somehow still satisfying, to get these thoughts down.

And who knows, it is on the internet, and there _are_ search engines, so maybe, somehow, someday, someone might actually read this stuff!?
